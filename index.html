Expert Avian Care Guide & Advisory

Keeping a companion bird is a lifetime relationship. Follow our expert tips to keep them singing and physically fit at your home.

Cage Sizing & Spatial Needs

Birds need plenty of wing extension room. A cage should be at least three times the wing-span of your bird.

Diet Balance Formulation

Never rely solely on seeds. Supply a healthy balance of premium pellets, fresh greens (kale, broccoli), and fresh water daily.

Mental Stimulation & Play

Birds are highly cognitive creatures. Rotate wooden chew toys, swings, and foraging mirrors every 2-3 weeks to avoid depression.
